## Current Quick-fire Q&A

These are the existing questions and draft answers on the site, ready for you to rewrite in your own voice.

### The camera in your pocket

Current position: before **Saying goodbye to my Audi TT.**.

**Does a phone photo count?**
Of course. Some of my favourite small moments here were taken on a phone.

**Why keep the camera details?**
I like remembering what I had with me when a picture happened, whether that was my Sony or my iPhone.

### Dogs and dog shows

Current position: before **A seal at Exploris**.

**Have you ever shown a dog?**
I competed in conformation shows for a short while. I enjoyed the training, but these days I prefer watching from the sidelines with my camera.

**A breed you always come back to?**
Australian Shepherds. I fell down a very deep rabbit hole learning about them.

**Any other favourites?**
Hovawarts and Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retrievers are up there too.

### Behind the pictures

Current position: before **Peregrine**.

**Which lens do you reach for most?**
My Sony 55mm f/1.8. It’s gorgeously sharp, gives me beautiful depth of field and I like the constraint of zooming with my feet.

**What do you most enjoy photographing now?**
Animals, always—but I’m also enjoying exploring automotive photography and finding a new visual language for it.

**What got you into photography?**
Wanting to hold onto the small details: live music, animals, journeys and the ordinary scenes that become meaningful later.
